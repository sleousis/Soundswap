# Changelog

Each release's section is shown in Dalamud's plugin installer and on the GitHub release page. The release
workflow refuses a tag whose version has no section here.

## 0.1.0

- Make a music mod without leaving the game: pick any of the game's songs, drop in your own, and Build & install puts it in Penumbra and switches it on.
- Song names come from Orchestrion's list, live when Orchestrion is installed. With it you can also press Replace on what is playing right now, and hear your mod straight after building.
- Reads MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC, M4A, AAC, MP4, WMA and AIFF. The volume is matched to the song it replaces, and the loop is taken from the file's loop tags or set by you.
- Layered songs (the ones the game fades between during a fight) get a choice per layer: your song, silence, or the game's own layer.
- Hear everything before building: the game's version, each of its layers, your song as it will play, and the loop seam.
- Export a .pmp to share or keep.
